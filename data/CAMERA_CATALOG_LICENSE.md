# Camera catalog attribution

`camera_catalog.json` is generated from the
[Lensfun camera database](https://github.com/lensfun/lensfun) and includes
locally curated FrameWise entries.

The Lensfun database is copyright its contributors and licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

FrameWise modifies the source data by normalizing manufacturer names,
excluding non-camera device makers, deduplicating models, sorting the catalog,
and adding locally curated camera names. The generated catalog is distributed
under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 terms.
